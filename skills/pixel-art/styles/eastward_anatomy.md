# Eastward anatomy library (faces, hands, legs)

Real sprites printed with `tools/refs.py text --map styles/eastward_palette.json`, so shared colors always use the same letter:
`o` ink, `b` shadow brown, `s` skin shadow, `S`/`K` skin, `d` deep red-brown, `W`/`w` cream, `u` muted violet, `g` olive-grey, `e` sage, `t` tan, `n` brown-orange, `y` ochre.
Digits are that sprite's own colors.

## Grammar

- **Inside the face there is almost no ink.**
  - Eyes are single `o` pixels, or `b` pixels, or hidden in socket shapes (`s` or `u`).
  - The mouth, brows, nose underside and age lines are shadow brown `b`.
  - The nose, sockets, cheeks and jaw are shaped with skin shadow `s`.
  - Ink draws the head's outer contour, and on fat faces the jowls.
- **A large cast shadow sits under the chin**: 1–3 rows of `b` or `s` across the neck and collar.
- **Age and character come from marks, not from a different head shape:**
  - nasolabial `b` lines, under-eye bags (cool `u`/blue-grey), jowls, double chin;
  - heavy brows, big beards, grey patches in the hair.
  - A round face with a closed-eye grin and no marks reads as a child.
- **Faces are wide** (13–17 px on adults) with the features clustered in the middle. Eyes are 3–5 px apart, the nose sits 1–2 rows below them, and the mouth 2 rows below that.
- **Hands** are inked mittens: a skin base, an `s` underside, and sometimes one ink split for the thumb. Big characters get thick forearms.
- **Legs show under every garment.** Use two separate feet or boots, 3–5 px wide each, with a dark sole row, separated by an ink line or a gap. A figure that stops at a hem on top of boot slabs reads as a box.

## Seen from in front and above: rings are arcs, tops show

Every horizontal ring around a form (hem, belt, waistband, cuff, collar, hat band or brim, boot top, rim) is the near half of an ellipse. It dips toward the viewer in the middle and rises at the sides. Tops of forms show, and the bottoms hide.
