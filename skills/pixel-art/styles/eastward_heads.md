# Eastward head library

Real heads (top 45 % of each sprite) printed with `--map eastward_palette.json`, so shared colors keep fixed letters:
`o` ink, `b` shadow brown, `s` skin shadow, `S`/`K` skin, `d` deep red-brown, `W`/`w` cream, `u` muted violet,
`g` olive-grey, `e` sage, `t` tan, `n` brown-orange, `y` ochre. Digits are that sprite's own colors.

**How to use:**
1. Pick the head closest to your character's type.
2. Adapt its structure, keeping these where they sit and at their size:
   - the outline bumps: ears, jowls, hair tufts, hat brim;
   - the shadow side and the band under the hair or hat;
   - the eye-socket band merging into the brows;
   - the nose shape and its dark underside;
   - the mouth, and the shadows under the lip and the chin.
3. Change hair, headwear, features and colors to make your own character. Don't copy pixels.
